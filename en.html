Retaking a course

📱E-attendance and excused absences
1. Install the e-attendance app (Yuhan Attendance)

To check in to classes and apply for excused absences, be sure to install the app for your smartphone model(after the first login, you are logged in automatically).

App Store Google Play
  • After the professor starts the class in the app, open the app and check in via Bluetooth.
  • Simple attendance corrections: If you were marked late/absent due to a system error, etc., contact your professor directlyto request an offline correction. (Applying for an excused absence in the app will be rejected.)
2. Excused absences and reserve forces training

When you are absent, attach the supporting documents required by the class operation guidelines in the attendance app; the absence is recognized after the professor approves it

[Reserve forces mobilization/training]
  • Courses using e-attendance: Apply through the attendance app (attach the 【reserve forces training certificate】) → once the professor approves, attendance is recognized automatically ('○')
  • Courses not using e-attendance(intensive courses, capstone design, etc.): submit the certificate directly to each course's professor → offline attendance record corrected
[Notes on excused absences (recognized attendance)]
  • Excused hours are recognized as "attendance" only when they are within 1/4 of total class hours
  • If absent hours including excused absences exceed 1/4 of total class hours, no credit will be given (F or NP) , so please be careful.
🎓3. Graduation Requirements
Graduation requirements (associate degree)
2-year programs: Complete 8+ general education credits, 60+ major credits, and 72+ total credits
3-year programs: Complete 12+ general education credits, 90+ major credits, and 108+ total credits
All required major and required general education courses must be completed
From the 2026 entering class, 2 credits from English courses designated by the Basic Convergence Education Center are required to graduate
📊4. Grading
Evaluation method

Grades are based on a comprehensive evaluation of attendance, assignments, exam scores, etc. (out of 100)

All courses are graded on an absolute scale in principle, and the proportion of A grades per course does not exceed 40% (excluding PASS-graded courses)

📋6. Changes in Academic Status
  • Eligibility: Students unable to attend more than 1/4 of total class days due to personal circumstances, illness or other unavoidable reasons
  • Application period: Apply from the leave application period of each semester until before final exams
  • Leave period: You may apply up to 4 times while enrolled; leave is not allowed in the 1st semester of the 1st year
Procedure: online leave application
1
Log in to the Yuhan University portal system
2
Click Integrated Information System
3
Click Academic Records - General leave / Military leave (change period)
4
Read the online leave application notes carefully and click Confirm
5
Select the leave subtype → click Save leave application

(For military, illness, pregnancy/childbirth/childcare and start-up leave, upload supporting documents)

6
Check the leave application details and click Next
7
Agree to the collection and use of personal information, check your contact details, then click Next
8
Complete the survey (general leave only) to finish the application
Additional tuition when taking leave

If you take general leave after paying tuition, you must pay tuition as follows when you return

1
Leave taken within 30 days of the semester start

No additional tuition

2
Leave taken from day 31 to day 60 after the semester start

Pay an additional 1/3 of tuition for the semester of return

3
Leave taken from day 61 to day 90 after the semester start

Pay an additional 1/2 of tuition for the semester of return

4
Leave taken after day 90 from the semester start

Pay the full tuition again for the semester of return

Students who were readmitted or changed majors cannot take leave in that semester (except military leave)
  • Eligibility: Students who received an enlistment notice
  • Application period: Apply online from 10 days before enlistment until enlistment
  • Leave period: Applied according to enlistment date and service period
  • Required documents: 1 copy of the enlistment notice, confirmation of (scheduled) enlistment, or certificate of military service
  • Grade processing: If the enlistment date falls in weeks 13–14 of class days, you may apply for grade substitution at your discretion; if it falls in week 15 or later, grades up to that point are converted into the semester's grades regardless of your wishes and the semester is considered completed
  • Students on general leave who wish to extend their leave due to military enlistment can apply online to change the leave period via the Integrated Information System (from 10 days before the enlistment date)
  • Required documents: 1 copy of the enlistment notice or certificate of military service
  • Eligibility: Students on leave must return when the leave period ends; students who do not return after the leave period expires will be expelled
  • Eligibility: Students who enlisted while on general leave without going through the military leave procedure and were expelled
  • Application period: You may apply for reinstatement within 1 year of discharge
  • Required documents: Military service documents (certificate of military service, military service confirmation, etc.)
  • Other: The same applies if the service period was extended as a short-term non-commissioned officer
  • Students who wish to withdraw to attend another university or for other unavoidable reasons must consult their academic advisor and department head, then fill out a withdrawal form and submit it to the Academic Support Team, Office of Academic Affairs
  • Tuition refund: If you withdraw while enrolled, the withdrawal approval date is the reference date for the tuition refund; if you withdraw while on leave, the leave application date is the reference date (refunded under the 「Regulations on University Tuition」)
  • Required documents: 1 withdrawal form, your seal, guardian's seal (must be brought when submitting the form), copy of a bank book in your name (if a tuition refund applies)

02 Scholarships·Student Loans

Scholarship information from on-campus scholarships to the National Scholarship

🏫1. On-campus Scholarships
Yuhan Foundation Chairman Scholarship
One top student per department is selected each semester and year and receives full tuition as a scholarship. Recipients rotate by department and are finally selected by the Scholarship Committee
Yuhan Foundation Scholarship
A scholarship for freshmen who graduated from Yuhan Technical High School with excellent grades and were recommended by the principal
Freshman Merit Scholarship
A scholarship deducted in advance from the tuition bill for students with excellent overall/admission scores (KRW 1,000,000)
Merit Scholarship
Departments select students with excellent grades; a scholarship is deducted in advance from the tuition bill
Contribution Scholarship
A scholarship deducted in advance from the tuition bill for students recognized for outstanding contributions to the university (KRW 350,000)
Student Leader Scholarship
A scholarship for student council members, representatives and department student leaders with a GPA of 2.50 or higher, after review by the Scholarship Committee
Yuhan Hope Scholarship (low-income scholarship)
A scholarship for students with a GPA of 2.0 or higher facing financial hardship (eligible income brackets and amounts depend on the university budget)
Faculty & Staff Children Scholarship
Tuition is paid as a scholarship to children of current university faculty and staff
Faculty & Staff Spouse·Sibling Scholarship
A scholarship for spouses and siblings of current faculty and staff with a GPA of 2.50 or higher
Spouse, Family and Sibling Scholarship
If two or more spouses, direct family members or siblings are enrolled at the same time and have a GPA of 2.50 or higher, 30% of tuition is paid as a scholarship
On-campus Work Scholarship
A monthly scholarship for diligent students who serve in their department as work scholars
Reporter Scholarship
A monthly scholarship for students selected as PR scholars of the university's Media & PR Center
International Student Scholarship
Scholarships for international students admitted through special admission for overseas Koreans/foreigners, differentiated by TOPIK level (up to 60% of tuition)
Industry-commissioned Scholarship
A scholarship for the first semester only for students admitted through industry-commissioned admission (up to 30% of tuition)
Yuhan Love Scholarship (students with disabilities)
A scholarship for students with disability grade 1–3 under the Act on Welfare of Persons with Disabilities and a GPA of 2.00 or higher (30% of tuition)
Yuhan Global Scholarship
A scholarship for students from multicultural families with a GPA of 2.50 or higher (30% of tuition)
Veterans Scholarship
A scholarship for persons of national merit (or their children) who submit a certificate from the Ministry of Patriots and Veterans Affairs, after review by the Scholarship Committee under the Ministry's scholarship regulations
North Korean Defector Scholarship
A scholarship for North Korean defectors (or their children) who submit a certificate of eligibility for educational protection, under the Act on the Protection and Settlement Support of North Korean Defectors
Work-Study Dual (Junior College Stage) Special Scholarship
A scholarship for students participating in the junior college work-study dual program
P-TECH Special Scholarship
Students admitted through P-TECH special admission receive full tuition excluding government support
Special Scholarship
Operated temporarily to support students' tuition in case of national disasters or at the president's discretion
Yuhan STEP-UP Scholarship
A scholarship for students on academic probation (GPA below 1.5) who substantially improved their grades (GPA 4.0 or higher)
Alumni Children Scholarship
A scholarship for one semester for children of Yuhan University graduates who enroll (up to 30% of tuition)
Grades are based on the previous semester; scholarship budgets and amounts may change every year

📱 E-Attendance System Guide

How to use the e-attendance app for systematic academic management

ℹ️General information
Available from the first day of the semester — all faculty and students (including freshmen) should know this
How to install the app

Please install the Yuhan Attendance (student) app for your smartphone model.

App Store Google Play
If the app is not the latest version, be sure to update it before use.
Key steps
  • After the first login, you are logged in automatically
  • Excused absence: Attach supporting documents in the attendance app → professor approves → applied automatically
  • Reserve forces attendance: Attach the training certificate in the attendance app → professor approves → attendance (○) recognized
👨‍🏫For professors
APP — Start Class button
1
Click the "Start Class" button when class begins

Clickable time: From 9 minutes before to 14 min 59 sec after class start

2
Correct any student attendance errors during class

This must be done within class time.

⚠️ If the button is not clicked within 15 minutes of class start, the class is recorded as cancelled
Professors may not shorten or change class time arbitrarily (a repeated administrative audit finding)
WEB — How to check the attendance record
  • Temporary attendance record: Integrated Information System → Courses → Reports → Temporary attendance record
  • E-attendance record: Log in to Yuhan Portal → [Go to e-attendance] at the bottom → course [Attendance record] → check absent/excused hours
💡 If absent hours + excused hours exceed 1/4 of total class hours, no credit is given (F or NP)
🎓For students
How to check in
1
Open the attendance app during class → click the "Check in" button

Do this with Bluetooth + internet (mobile data recommended) turned on

2
Back-to-back classes

Same classroom: click only at the start
Different classroom (theory + practice, etc.): click again after changing classrooms

Attendance rules
CategoryTime
PresentFrom 4 min before class start to 4 min 59 sec after class start
LateAfter class start, 5 min ~ 19 min 59 sec
AbsentAfter class start, After 20 min
There is no check-out, but professors may do a surprise check (mid-class attendance check).

✅ Excused Absence

How to apply for attendance changes (excused absence) for reserve forces, illness, family events, etc.

📋Type of application
CategoryReason for absenceHow to apply
Excused absence Reasons for absence specified in Article 30 of the Enforcement Rules of the University Regulations E-attendance app → apply for excused absence + attach supporting documents
Simple attendance correction Errors marking a present student late/absent (mistakes, system errors, etc.) Contact each course's professor directly (app applications will be rejected)
⚠️ If absent hours including excused absences exceed 1/4 of total class hours, no credit is given (F or NP)
Excused hours, including absent hours, may not exceed 1/4 of total class hours per course
🪖How to apply for reserve forces training
Reserve forces training is treated as "attendance (○)", not as an excused absence.
Courses using e-attendance
1
For back-to-back classes, confirm all hours 1·2·3 show "absent", then
2
E-attendance app → apply for reserve forces recognition

Attaching the 【reserve forces training certificate】 is required

3
Professor approves → attendance (○) applied automatically
Courses not using e-attendance

For intensive courses, team teaching, capstone design, step-by-step, etc., submit the training certificate directly to the professor → the professor corrects the offline attendance record

Type of trainingScope

Mobilization-exempt training3 days 2 nights or 32 hours

If you can't see the reserve forces menu, check whether the e-attendance app is up to date.
